Difference or disorder? Start with the child's whole language life

A child who speaks Telugu at home and is learning Kannada and English at school will look delayed on any single-language test. This profile records how much of each language the child hears and the signs that point to a true language disorder, which shows up in every language the child uses.

Child and languages

For each language, estimate the hours in a typical waking week the child hears it (from family, school, carers and media) and when the child first heard it.

Signs to ask about

Ask the family about the child's strongest or home language, not the school language.
